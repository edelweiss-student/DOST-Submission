Keybinds

        

          

            
Moving

            

              
WASD
Move, swim

              
Mouse
Look

              
Shift
Sprint, swim fast (uses stamina)

              
Space
Jump, swim up

              
C
Dive

            

          

          

            
Hands and tools

            

              
E
With your hands: gather, pick up, build and repair

              
Right-click
Use the selected item: swing a tool (chop, split logs, cut, break what’s built), eat, or put up your team’s bahay kubo or bed

              
Hold Right-click
With the spear: draw it back (the meter fills), let go to throw it

              
R
Turn the bed or bahay kubo before you place it

              
1–8Wheel
Choose a hotbar slot

              
Q
Drop one of the selected item, or put down wood

              
Hold X
Take apart what you built

            

          

          

            
Building

            

              
B
Build mode on or off

              
EClick
Place the blueprint

              
1–8Wheel
Choose a piece

              
R
Turn the piece: a floor’s boards, a roof’s ridge, the braces on pilings, or a wall, railing or stairs round the floor

              
Z
Raise or lower pilings (how high their deck stands)

              
Right-click
Leave build mode

              
E on a build
Add wood, repair it, open or shut a door, or work its joints

            

          

          

            
Fighting (multiplayer)

            

              
ClickF
Strike with what’s in your hand: other teams’ players and beds, and what’s built
